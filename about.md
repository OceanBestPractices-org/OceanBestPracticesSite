### History
The Ocean Best Practices System (OBPS) was launched in 2017 to enhance the management of ocean methods. It is a project co-sponsored by the **Global Ocean Observing System (GOOS)** and the **IODE**.

### Mission
To sustain an evolving system which fosters collaboration, consensus-building, and innovation by providing coordinated and global access to best practices and standards across ocean sciences and applications.

### Organization
Hosted by the UNESCO/IOC Project Office for IODE in Oostende, Belgium.
