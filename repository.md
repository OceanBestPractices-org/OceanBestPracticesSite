The IOC-OBPS Repository is a secure, permanent, and searchable platform for ocean practices. It is a global open-access resource serving the needs of a broad range of ocean stakeholders.

### How to use the Repository
1.  **Search:** [Access the Repository Search Engine](https://search.oceanbestpractices.org)
2.  **Submit:** Register for a free account to submit your own methodological documents.
3.  **Endorsement:** Learn how to get your practice "GOOS Endorsed."

### Policy & Terms
The repository is hosted by the **International Oceanographic Data and Information Exchange (IODE)**. Content includes protocols, guidelines, and standard operating procedures.
