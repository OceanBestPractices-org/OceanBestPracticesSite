The OBPS leads a dedicated Research Topic, **"Best Practices in Ocean Observing,"** in the open-access journal *Frontiers in Marine Science*.

- **Promoting Visibility:** This journal highlights community-vetted methodologies.
- **Cross-Archiving:** All published articles are linked to the full technical documentation in the OBPS Repository.
- **Submit:** We encourage submissions of protocols, technology reports, and code descriptions to this journal.
